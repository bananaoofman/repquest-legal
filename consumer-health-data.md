# Consumer health data in the US

Washington’s My Health My Data Act, Nevada’s similar law and others protect “consumer health data”. In RepQuest that can include your workouts, body weight, height, steps, goals and how experienced you are. We collect it from you and your phone’s sensors, only to run RepQuest. It leaves your phone only if you back it up, when it goes only to Supabase, which stores it for us, or if you share it yourself in a workout image or export. We never sell it and share it with no one else unless the law requires it.

You can confirm whether we hold it, see it, delete it and withdraw your consent to its backup, as Your rights and Your choices describe. When you ask what we hold, we also tell you every company we shared it with and how to contact it. We answer within 45 days. If we refuse a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer; we answer an appeal within 45 days and, if we refuse it, tell you how to complain to your state’s attorney general (in Washington, at atg.wa.gov).
