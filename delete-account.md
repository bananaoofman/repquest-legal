# Delete your RepQuest account

RepQuest is made by MujarStudios. You don't need an account to use RepQuest. An account only exists if you signed in to back up your progress.

## Delete it in the app

1. Open **Settings → Account & backup**.
2. Choose **Delete account and cloud data** and confirm.
3. Enter the code we email to your account's address. This makes sure it's you, so nobody else holding your phone can delete your account.

This permanently deletes your account and its data in the app's cloud backup at once: workouts, routines, exercises, your hero and game progress, and settings. It can't be undone. (Copies in our database provider's logs and backups expire later, as below.)

It also removes the account's data from the phone you delete it on, and its exported files and workout images there. The phone then goes back to its guest data, which stays until you erase it in **Settings → Your data**. On other phones signed in to the account, its copy stays until you sign out there and choose **Remove from this device**.

If our terms have changed and you have not accepted them yet, the screen that asks you to accept them leads to **Delete your account** too.

## Delete it without the app

If you no longer have the app, email **mujarstudios@gmail.com** from the email address you signed in with, asking to delete your RepQuest account. We will delete the account and all of its backed-up data within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is kept

Deleting the account removes it and its data from the live cloud database at once, and its purchase record at RevenueCat, our subscription provider. Afterwards:

- Our database provider's records of sign-ins and requests (which can hold your email address or IP address), and the copies in its backups, are deleted as they expire, within 30 days.
- Google Play and the App Store keep their own records of any purchase. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in the store.
- Sign-in code emails are deleted from our email account within 30 days of being sent. Emails you sent us are kept for up to two years after our last reply, and longer only if a legal claim needs them.
- The stores' sales reports, and other records the law requires us to keep, such as for tax, are kept for as long as that law requires. They hold no name or email address.

The app has no analytics or advertising data to keep. See the privacy policy for the details.
